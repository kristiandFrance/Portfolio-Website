## What it is

**You're Here Again** is a weirdcore, fast-paced movement shooter set in the
liminal space between death and whatever follows. It was published by
**Devious Devs** and released into Early Access on Steam on
**13 December 2025**, where it's still being updated.

The game is built around momentum: wall-running, sliding, grappling and
slamming chain together, speed carries between moves, and damage scales with
velocity — the faster you're moving, the harder you hit.

## The shape of it

- **33 levels** across the campaign, with **3 bosses** and **10+ enemy types**
- A **level editor with Steam Workshop** support, **leaderboards** and
  **cloud saves**
- **7 Steam achievements** and a **soundtrack DLC**
- Shipped in **four languages**: English, Simplified Chinese, Russian and
  Te Reo Māori

## What I owned

I was one of a five-developer team at Devious Devs, credited on the Steam
store page as `yodapowers_`. My areas:

- **Weapon systems** — the guns, how they feel, how they interact with the
  movement kit
- **AI systems** — enemy behaviour across the 10+ enemy types
- **Audio direction, SFX and music** — direction for the game's sound as
  well as authoring
- **VFX systems** — the systems that drive visual feedback
- **In-game feedback and bug-reporting pipeline** — players can report
  issues from inside the game, which is a large part of how a small team
  keeps an Early Access title honest

## After launch

Shipping was the halfway point. Since release I've worked on post-launch
updates, the soundtrack DLC, optimisation passes and the CI/CD pipeline that
lets a five-person team ship fixes quickly.
